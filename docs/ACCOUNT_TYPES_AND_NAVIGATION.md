# COLLIVRA account types and navigation

COLLIVRA has exactly two account types: **Student** and **Event Organiser**. The account type is selected during signup and is not selectable again in profile setup. Email address does not determine the account type.

## Student experience

The student experience is centred on collaboration and discovery:

- Home
- Discover people and projects
- Community and messages
- Start a project
- Discover and join events
- Profile and plans & billing

Event participation does not require an organiser subscription.

## Event Organiser experience

An Event Organiser owns events directly through their account. No workspace creation, staff invitation or workspace role setup is required. Organisers create events, configure registration and workflows, purchase an Event Pass, publish events, manage participants and operate event stages.

## Billing

- Student Premium: $2.99/month or $24.99/year.
- Event Organiser: $19/month or $190/year.
- Enterprise: custom contract.
- Event Pass: $9 Standard, $19 Advanced or $39 Complex, charged once per event and separately from subscriptions.

Legacy `institution_*`, `organisation_*` and `college_yearly` subscription keys remain readable for historical entitlements. New organiser subscriptions use `organiser_monthly` and `organiser_yearly`; historical subscription and payment records are not rewritten by the migration.
